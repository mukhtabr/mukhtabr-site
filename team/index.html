CARTOGRAPHA PARTICIPATORY MAPPING PROGRAMME

  1. 1

    Think of a Place

    Start with places that interest you or hold meaning to you.

  2. 2

    Map It Your Way

    Explore and interpret the place through your own eyes and perspective.

  3. 3

    Submit Your Map

    Share your personal representation of the place with your map.

There are no rules to follow and nothing has to be technically correct. Include whatever you want, leave out whatever you like, and think outside the box.

Choose your Format
  • Grids1–5 · 1/2 in, 1/4 in, 2 mm, 5 mm, 10 mm
  • Maps6–22 · Oman, governorates and Muscat wilayats
  • General Submissions23 · Blank page
    Download

All forms of submission are welcome, including photographs, audio recordings, drawings, sketches, and other creative interpretations.

Need ideas?
  • Draw on one of our sheets, your own paper, or send a photo of something you made.
  • Mark what matters to you: routes, sounds, smells, memories, people or landmarks.
  • Give your map a title and a few words so we can understand your place.
  • Hand your sheet in at H1 Art Gallery, or upload it on the Submit here page.
